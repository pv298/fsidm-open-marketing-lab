# Local SEO checklist

## Business profile
- [ ] Business name, address and phone number are accurate.
- [ ] Primary and secondary categories accurately describe the business.
- [ ] Opening hours and special hours are current.
- [ ] Services/products and business description are complete.
- [ ] Photos are authentic, current and representative.

## Website
- [ ] Location/service pages provide genuinely useful local information.
- [ ] Name, address and phone details are consistent.
- [ ] Contact details and directions are easy to find.
- [ ] Relevant local business structured data is accurate.

## Reputation
- [ ] A compliant process exists for requesting genuine customer reviews.
- [ ] Reviews receive timely, professional responses.
- [ ] Feedback themes are reviewed to improve service.
- [ ] No incentives or fabricated reviews are used.

## Measurement
- [ ] Calls, direction requests and website actions are monitored.
- [ ] Lead quality is reviewed, not just lead volume.
- [ ] Changes are documented with dates.
